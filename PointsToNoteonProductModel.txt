When designing the **Product** model for an e-commerce catalog service, you need to account for a variety of fields that will adequately represent a product, along with its properties for management, display, and searching.

Here’s a comprehensive list of the fields that could be included in the **Product** model:

### Basic Product Information:
1. **id** (String or Long)  
   - A unique identifier for the product (usually auto-generated or UUID).

2. **name** (String)  
   - The name of the product (e.g., "Apple iPhone 13").

3. **description** (String)  
   - A detailed description of the product, including features, benefits, and any other relevant information.

4. **brand** (String)  
   - The brand or manufacturer of the product (e.g., "Apple", "Samsung").

5. **SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)** (String)  
   - A unique identifier for each product variant used internally for inventory management.

6. **category** (String or List of Strings)  
   - The category or categories under which the product is classified (e.g., "Electronics", "Smartphones", "Home Appliances").  
   - Could also be a list of category IDs if using hierarchical categories.

7. **sub-category** (String or List of Strings)  
   - A more specific categorization (e.g., "Smartphones" under "Electronics").

8. **price** (BigDecimal or Double)  
   - The price of the product.

9. **discountPrice** (BigDecimal or Double, optional)  
   - The discounted price if the product is on sale.

10. **currency** (String)  
    - Currency of the price, e.g., "USD", "EUR".

11. **stockQuantity** (Integer or Long)  
    - The number of units available in inventory.

12. **weight** (BigDecimal or Double)  
    - The weight of the product (for shipping calculations).

13. **dimensions** (String or JSON object with length, width, height)  
    - The dimensions of the product (e.g., "6.1 x 2.8 x 0.3 inches").

14. **tags** (List of Strings)  
    - Tags or keywords for better searchability (e.g., "wireless", "fast charging", "smartphone").

15. **createdAt** (Date or LocalDateTime)  
    - The date and time when the product was added to the catalog.

16. **updatedAt** (Date or LocalDateTime)  
    - The date and time when the product details were last updated.

### Media and Visual Information:
17. **images** (List of Strings or URLs)  
    - URLs to images associated with the product (e.g., main product image, additional images for variations).

18. **videos** (List of URLs, optional)  
    - Links to product demonstration videos or promotional videos.

19. **thumbnail** (String, optional)  
    - URL for a small-sized image to be used as a thumbnail.

### Product Variants (Optional but Useful):
20. **variants** (List of Variant objects)  
    - Variants of the product, for example, different sizes or colors. Each variant could have its own SKU, price, stock quantity, and specific attributes.

    Example fields inside a variant:
    - **variantId** (String or Long)  
    - **color** (String)  
    - **size** (String)  
    - **material** (String)  
    - **additionalPrice** (BigDecimal or Double) (if the variant has a different price)

### Pricing & Promotional Information:
21. **originalPrice** (BigDecimal or Double)  
    - The original price of the product before any discounts or promotions.

22. **saleStartDate** (Date or LocalDateTime, optional)  
    - Start date of any ongoing sale or promotion.

23. **saleEndDate** (Date or LocalDateTime, optional)  
    - End date of any ongoing sale or promotion.

24. **isOnSale** (Boolean)  
    - Boolean flag indicating if the product is currently on sale.

25. **discountPercentage** (BigDecimal, optional)  
    - Discount percentage if the product is on discount (useful for calculating the sale price).

### Product Ratings & Reviews:
26. **averageRating** (Float or Double, optional)  
    - The average rating for the product, calculated from customer reviews (e.g., 4.5/5).

27. **ratingCount** (Integer, optional)  
    - The total number of ratings the product has received.

28. **reviews** (List of Review objects, optional)  
    - A list of customer reviews for the product, each with details such as text, user rating, and timestamps.

### Availability & Shipping:
29. **shippingDetails** (ShippingDetails object)  
    - Shipping-related information, such as estimated delivery time, available shipping methods, and costs.

30. **isAvailable** (Boolean)  
    - A flag indicating whether the product is available for purchase (could be linked to stockQuantity).

31. **shippingWeight** (BigDecimal or Double)  
    - Weight of the product including packaging, for calculating shipping costs.

32. **isPreOrder** (Boolean)  
    - A flag indicating whether the product is available for pre-order.

### SEO and Marketing Information:
33. **metaTitle** (String, optional)  
    - Meta title for SEO purposes (shown on search engines).

34. **metaDescription** (String, optional)  
    - Meta description for SEO purposes.

35. **seoTags** (List of Strings, optional)  
    - SEO tags for better visibility on search engines.

36. **brandLogo** (String, optional)  
    - URL to the brand’s logo for display purposes.

### Miscellaneous:
37. **productType** (String)  
    - Type of the product (e.g., "physical", "digital", "service").

38. **status** (String, e.g., "active", "inactive", "discontinued")  
    - The status of the product (whether it's currently available for sale).

39. **warranty** (String, optional)  
    - Warranty information (e.g., "2 years", "1 year limited").

40. **originCountry** (String)  
    - The country where the product was manufactured.

41. **supplierId** (String or Long, optional)  
    - Identifier for the supplier of the product.
